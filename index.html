02 — Understand

The climate challenge.

Climate change affects the planet in many connected ways. Understanding the problem helps us choose better solutions.

🌡️

Heat

Greenhouse gases trap heat in the atmosphere, contributing to long-term warming.

🌊

Water & oceans

Warming can influence sea levels, rainfall patterns and ocean ecosystems.

🌳

Nature

Plants and animals depend on climate conditions, habitats and available resources.

03 — Explore

Green technology toolbox.

Tap a technology to reveal how it works and how it can contribute to a cleaner future.

☀️

Solar Power

Sunlight → electricity.

Photovoltaic cells convert light into electrical energy. Solar systems can generate power without fuel combustion during operation.

🌬️

Wind Energy

Moving air → electricity.

Wind turns turbine blades connected to a generator. Wind is renewable, although output depends on conditions.

🚗

Electric Vehicles

Battery → electric motor.

Electric motors use stored electricity. Their overall climate benefit depends partly on how the electricity is produced.

🏙️

Smart Cities

Data → smarter systems.

Sensors and software can help manage traffic, lighting, buildings, energy and water more efficiently.

💧

Water Tech

Monitor → save.

Smart meters, sensors and efficient treatment systems can detect problems and reduce unnecessary water use.

♻️

Recycling

Waste → resource.

Sorting and processing can recover useful materials for reuse, reducing waste and demand for some new resources.
